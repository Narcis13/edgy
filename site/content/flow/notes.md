The Notes view shows your notes as a grid of cards, pinned ones first, for the things you want to keep and find again.

## Writing a note

**New note** at the top of the view takes a title and, under it, as many lines as you like; `#tags` on the first line are read, and the rest is kept as written. A note posted from the timeline's composer is the same note. In the grid a card shows the title, the first lines of the body, the tags, and a pin when it is pinned.

::shot flow-notes "The notes, the pinned one first."

## Editing

Click a card to edit it in place: the title and the body become fields, and clicking outside, or pressing Esc, saves. The **⋯** menu on a card pins or unpins it, makes it a task (it leaves the grid and appears in the board's first column) or an event, opens the full editor, or deletes it (after asking).

## Finding a note

The search box filters the grid by words in the title, the body and the tags; click a tag on a card to see only notes with that tag. Notes also show in the timeline, where they can be commented on and reacted to like anything else.
