You make three small documents, group them into a deck in the library, put them in order and play them as slides. It takes about twenty minutes. Read [A quote that adds itself up](/tutorials/quote/) first for the keys used to split cells.

A [deck](term:deck) is not a bigger document. Each slide is a whole document that stays what it was; the deck only keeps them together in an order. You will use the [library](/library/), a deck's page and **Play**.

## Slide 1: the title

1. On the home page, press **New document**. Click the title at the top, select all (⌘A, or Ctrl+A), type `Q4 review: title` and press Enter.
2. Click the empty cell and type `# Q4 review`. Press Esc.
3. Press [⌥↓](key:split) (Alt+↓ elsewhere). Type `Northwind Studio` and press Esc.
4. Click the logo at the top left (**All documents**) to go back to the home page.

## Slide 2: the numbers

5. Press **New document**. Set the title to `Q4 review: numbers` the same way. With no cell selected, set **Currency** in the **Page** section of **Cell details** to `EUR` and press Enter.
6. Click the empty cell and type `# Numbers`. Press Esc. Press ⌥↓, then `/`, type `Stat` and press Enter.
7. In **Cell details**, press **Default** beside **Trend** to take the sample line away. Then set **Number** to `41200`, **Label** to `Billed` and **Compare with** to `38000`, pressing Enter after each. To replace a field's text, click it, select all (⌘A, or Ctrl+A) and type.
8. Click the stat on the sheet so the keys go back to the sheet. Press ⌥→ (Alt+→), then `/` **Stat**. Press **Default** beside **Trend**. Set **Show the number as** to **Percent**. Set **Number** to `0.85`, **Label** to `On time` and **Compare with** to `0.8`.
9. Click the logo to go back to the home page.

## Slide 3: what is next

10. Press **New document**. Set the title to `Q4 review: next` the same way.
11. Click the empty cell and type `# Next`. Press Esc. Press ⌥↓, then `/`, type `Bulleted list` and press Enter. The cell shows two sample items.
12. Click the text of the first item, select it all and type `Hire a second developer`. Press Tab. Click the second item and type `Raise day rates`. Press Tab.
13. Click the logo to go back to the home page.

## Group them

14. On the home page, tick the box on the three cards: *Q4 review: title*, *Q4 review: numbers* and *Q4 review: next*. A bar appears at the bottom of the page.
15. Press **Group into a deck**. In the dialog, set **Title** to `Q4 review` and press **Group**.

::shot library "The library, with a deck's card among the documents."

The three documents leave the main list and sit inside the deck's card. They went in the order the home page showed them, which is newest first, so *next* is probably first.

## Put them in order

16. Click the title of the deck's card to open its page. The slides are listed in order, each with a small map of its document.
17. Use the arrows at the right of a slide, **Move up** and **Move down**, until the order is *title*, *numbers*, *next*. You can drag a slide by its grip instead.
18. In **Description**, type `The Q4 numbers in three slides` and press Enter.

::shot deck "The deck's page: its documents in order, with Play above them."

## Play

19. Press **Play**. The first slide fills the screen. Press → or click to the right of the slide to go to the next one, ← to go back, **F** for full screen and Esc to leave. Each slide is a live document: you can change a number or press a button on it.

::clip play "Playing a deck: the first slide, then the next, then leaving."

20. On the deck's page, **Export as HTML** saves the whole deck as one file that plays offline. See [Exporting](/library/export/).

## The finished document

::shot tutorial-deck "The middle slide, in Live: a heading and two stats."

The picture shows the slide you built in steps 5 to 8, the one with numbers on it.

You can open it as a template: the [Q4 review: numbers](template:tutorial-deck) entry on [Templates](/library/templates/) has an **Open in edgy** button. The deck itself is not a template, because a deck is not a document: you group documents you already have.

## What you learned

- Select two or more cards on the home page and choose **Group into a deck**. A document is in at most one deck. See [Decks and Play](/library/decks/).
- On a deck's page you reorder, add and take out documents, change the title and description, and press **Play**.
- **Play** shows each document live, one per slide: [→](key:play-next) and [←](key:play-previous) move, [F](key:play-full) is full screen and [Esc](key:play-leave) leaves.
- A deck exports as one file. See [Exporting](/library/export/).
